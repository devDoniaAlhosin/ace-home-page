What we do

Ten disciplines, one story. Follow a mosque from the first line on its site to the day it is handed over.

All ten areas of expertise

  1. Chapter 01Envision

    The site

    Master planning

    Every project starts on the ground. We read the plot, the access, the sun and, for a mosque, the direction of the qibla, then decide where the building sits.

    Explore master planning

  2. Chapter 02Envision

    The form

    Architectural design

    The prayer hall, the arcades, the portal and the minaret take shape: a form that belongs to its place and works for the people who use it.

    Explore architectural design

  3. Chapter 03Envision

    The setting

    Landscape design

    Palms, planting, paving and water make the approach part of the building, and give shade where people gather.

    Explore landscape design

  4. Chapter 04Envision

    The inside

    Interior design

    Inside, the prayer rows, the mihrab and the light are planned so the hall feels calm and works when it is full.

    Explore interior design

  5. Chapter 05Engineer

    The structure

    Engineering services

    Structural engineers set the column grid, the foundations and the load paths, and check the minaret against wind. MEP engineers plan the cooling and power.

    Explore engineering services

  6. Chapter 06Engineer

    The climate

    Sustainability

    We model sun, heat and air movement so the building stays cool on less energy, and support Estidama or LEED where a project needs it.

    Explore sustainability

  7. Chapter 07Coordinate

    The model

    BIM

    Every discipline meets in one BIM model. A duct that runs into a beam is found and fixed on screen, not on site.

    Explore BIM

  8. Chapter 08Coordinate

    The detail

    Digital intelligence

    Computational tools generate and test details like the geometric screens, and keep the whole team working from the same data.

    Explore digital intelligence

  9. Chapter 09Coordinate

    The programme

    Project management and AOR

    As project manager and Architect of Record, we steer design, authority approvals, tender and construction through to handover.

    Explore project management and AOR

  10. Chapter 10Deliver

    The build

    Site supervision

    On site, our supervisors check every stage against the drawings, the contract and the regulations, until the building is handed over.

    Explore site supervision
