Welcometo ACE

ACE is a global architectural and engineering consultancy delivering integrated, high-performance solutions across sectors. We design with purpose, engineer with precision, and build with a commitment to lasting impact.

Founded in 1975 in Al Ain, ACE has grown into a leading multidisciplinary consultancy, shaping skylines, transforming cities, and delivering high-performance, future-ready environments. Since then, we’ve evolved from local pioneers into trusted partners on large-scale, complex developments across the Middle East and beyond.

Today, ACE stands at the forefront of design and engineering innovation, offering integrated services in architecture, engineering, sustainability, project management, and digital transformation. Our legacy reflects leadership, growth, and a global vision for the built environment.
