Who we are

ACE is a global architectural and engineering consultancy delivering integrated, high-performance solutions across sectors.

Founded in 1975 in Al Ain, ACE has grown from local pioneers into a trusted partner on large-scale, complex developments across the Middle East and beyond. We design with purpose, engineer with precision, and build with a commitment to lasting impact.
